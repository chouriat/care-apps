# CareCircle Store Data Safety (Google Play) & App Privacy (Apple App Store)

Reference table compiled directly from the CareCircle codebase to fill in **Google Play Console > App content > Data safety** and **App Store Connect > App Privacy**.

---

## 1. Summary Answers for Both Stores

- **Does the app collect or share required user data?** Yes.
- **Is all collected user data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS / TLS for all Supabase, Anthropic, Google Gemini, RevenueCat, Sentry, PostHog, and Expo Push requests).
- **Do users have a way to request that their data be deleted?** Yes — both in-app (**Circle Settings > Delete account**) and via the web/email deletion instructions in `docs/legal/delete-account.md`.
- **Does the app use data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking (Apple ATT)?** **No.** No data is used for advertising, marketing attribution, or tracking across apps/websites owned by other companies.
- **Does the app collect location data?** **No.** Even in PostHog analytics, GeoIP lookup from IP addresses is explicitly disabled (`disableGeoip: true` in `apps/carecircle/src/features/observability/api.ts`).
