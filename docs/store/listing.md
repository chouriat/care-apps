## Full Description (1,945 characters; limit 4,000 characters)

When siblings and relatives share the care of an aging parent, the hardest part is knowing what has already been done today. Did someone give Dad his morning tablet? Who is driving Mum to her cardiology appointment on Thursday? What did the doctor say at the last visit, and which follow-up tasks still need to be booked?

CareCircle helps your family keep track of daily care in one shared place so everyone stays on the same page.

**What CareCircle does:**

- **One shared family circle with clear roles:** Create a circle for your parent in their local time zone and invite relatives with single-use links. Choose who can record and edit care items (Owner and Helpers) and who has read-only access to stay informed (Viewers).
- **Daily medication schedule and check-offs:** Add medications with daily, weekly, every-N-days, or as-needed schedules. On the Today screen, family helpers mark doses as Given or Skipped as they happen, and everyone in the circle sees the update right away. If a scheduled dose is not logged within 60 minutes of its due time, CareCircle marks it as Missed and sends a privacy-safe notification so the family can check in.
- **Scan a prescription label into a draft:** Take or choose a photo of a prescription label to pre-fill a draft with the medicine name, strength, dose text, and schedule. You review and confirm every field before saving.
- **Shared appointment calendar:** Keep track of upcoming doctor visits, locations, notes, and which family member is driving.
- **Shared care tasks:** Create and assign follow-up errands (like picking up a refill or booking a lab test), filter by family member, and see open tasks due today on the Today screen.
- **Doctor visit notes and summaries (CareCircle Family Plan):** Record up to 10 minutes of audio or type your notes from an appointment to create a draft summary, suggested follow-up tasks, and suggested medication changes. Nothing is saved until you review and accept or reject each suggestion.
- **Chronological care log and PDF export:** Review an append-only timeline of dose check-offs, medication updates, appointments, tasks, and visit notes. With the CareCircle Family Plan, export a clean PDF of up to 90 days of the care log to share or print.

**Free and Family Plans:**

- **Free Plan:** 1 care circle with up to 2 members, shared medication schedules, dose check-offs, prescription label scanning, shared calendar, shared tasks, and the in-app care log.
- **CareCircle Family Plan (optional subscription):** Invite up to 8 members to your circle, generate AI visit-note summaries from audio or typed notes, and export care log PDFs.

**Important notice:**
CareCircle supports family care coordination and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions or treatment decisions.
